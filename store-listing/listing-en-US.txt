App name:
Flip: Face Down to Silence

Short description:
Put your phone face down and it goes quiet. Pick it up and the sound comes back.

Full description:
Put your phone face down on a table and it goes quiet. Pick it up and the sound comes back. No buttons, no schedules, no remembering to turn Do Not Disturb off again.

HOW IT WORKS
• Face down, flat and still. Flip waits until the phone is lying face down on a surface and holding its angle, then turns on Do Not Disturb. A phone in a pocket or held in your hand doesn't count.
• Pick it up. The moment you lift it, the sound comes back.
• Two quick taps. A short haptic tick confirms each change, because the screen is against the table. You can turn them off.

WHAT STILL GETS THROUGH
• People: let your starred contacts ring, and repeat callers too.
• Apps: choose apps whose notifications still reach you while everything else is quiet.
• Alarms always ring.

SENSITIVITY
• Strict, Balanced or Relaxed, from "only a proper flat table" to "a cushion or a quick set-down".

YOUR DAY AND YOUR NIGHTS
• See today's quiet stretches on a timeline.
• See last night: when you put the phone down and when you picked it up again. Sleep can be turned off in Settings.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no ads, no analytics
• No internet permission, so nothing leaves your phone
• Notification content is never read, only which app posted it

Requires Android 14 or newer.

Foreground service declaration (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE):
Type: Special use
Description:
Flip continuously reads the accelerometer to detect when the phone is placed face down
and held still on a flat surface, and then turns on Do Not Disturb; it turns it off again
the moment the phone is picked up. This must keep running while the screen is off and the
app is not visible, because the user puts the phone down precisely when they are not
looking at it. The user starts it with the switch on the main screen, it shows a
persistent notification while running, and it can be stopped at any time from the app.
Deferring or interrupting it (e.g. via WorkManager) would leave the phone ringing on the
table or stuck in Do Not Disturb after it is picked up.
Video: a screen recording showing the phone being put face down, Do Not Disturb turning
on, and being picked up (media/flip-demo-music.mp4 can be uploaded to YouTube as unlisted).

Health apps declaration: Not a health app. (If asked because of the sleep view: it shows
only when the phone lay face down / screen off, no health data, no sensors on the body.)
